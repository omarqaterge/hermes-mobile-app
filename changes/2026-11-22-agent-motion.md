- **See Hermux at work: a light round the text box, ticks for finished steps.** While Hermux works on a chat a
  gold light travels round the text box and a faint gold glow sits behind the chat; Send turns into Stop with a small
  turn. While Hermux waits for your answer the light stands still in amber. The message you send lifts out of the text
  box; the running step shimmers, and a finished step draws a green tick (a failed one a red cross and a small shake);
  sub-agents in an opened group each get a thin lane that fills as they finish; the thinking dot turns while Hermux
  thinks; the reply's buttons settle in when it is done, with one light tick from the phone (none over the lock
  screen). Everything stops when the turn ends, stands still while the app is in the background or the part is off
  screen, is short fades under Animations Reduced and nothing under Off. Checked in a desktop browser at phone width
  in all three themes and all three animation levels; not yet tried on a phone.
