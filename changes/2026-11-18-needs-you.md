- **Chats that wait for your answer now stand out.** A chat that wants to run a command or asked you a question moves
  to a "Needs you" group at the top of the chat list, with an amber dot and a few words saying what it waits for, however
  long ago it asked. The menu button in the chat header turns amber too. Tap the row to open the chat and answer there;
  approvals still need your fingerprint and nothing can be allowed from the list. Checked in a desktop browser against a
  test copy of Hermux. Not yet tried on a phone.
- **A double tap can't approve the next request by accident.** When one approval or question follows another, the new one
  ignores Allow and other answers for a moment, so you see what it asks first. Deny always works at once. Checked in a
  desktop browser against a test copy of Hermux. Not yet tried on a phone.
