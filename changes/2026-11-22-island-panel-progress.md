- **Follow a chat at work from the top of the screen.** Tapping the working card now opens the dark panel on the
  chat's progress: the live step, a clock, the task list filling in as tasks finish (or a moving band when there is
  none), other chats that are busy, the app Hermux is using with Watch, and Stop, which shows Stopping and then
  Stopped. It closes by itself after 90 seconds untouched. When the turn ends while it is open it shows Done with the
  start of the reply; its Open chat clears Done from the chip. A permission request or a question arriving while the
  panel is open takes its place, and after you answer one, the next waiting takes its place. While Hermux is using an
  app for that chat, the working card still opens the live viewer.
- **Answers from a card are confirmed.** After Allow, Deny or an answer from a card's own button, the chip says
  "Allowed", "Denied" or "Sent" for two seconds.
