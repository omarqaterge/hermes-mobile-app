- **Dictation can run on your phone, offline.** Settings → Voice → Speech recognition offers speech-to-text models that run on the phone instead of the phone’s own recogniser: your voice never leaves the phone and it works without internet. Four English models can be downloaded, each card saying what it is good and weaker at: Moonshine Base (recommended, most accurate, with punctuation, 142 MB), Moonshine Tiny (44 MB, lightest), NeMo FastConformer and Zipformer (words appear while you speak). You can also use **your own model**: paste a Hugging Face link or pick the files on the phone (any sherpa-onnx model: Moonshine, Whisper, SenseVoice, or a Zipformer/NeMo/Parakeet transducer, in any language). Every model is checked on a test recording before it is used, and the card shows how many words it got right and how fast. Dictation and Live mode use the chosen model; if it can’t start, the phone’s recogniser takes over. The model only works while the microphone is on. The app download grows from about 3 MB to about 20 MB (the speech engine). Tried on Android 15 and Android 8.0 emulators: downloads, checks, adding a model by link and from a .zip, live dictation through the microphone, a damaged model and a crashing check; not yet on a real phone.
