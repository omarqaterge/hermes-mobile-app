- **Cards in replies can no longer read files on your phone by themselves.** A card's live data could read and search files with no approval and pass what it found on to a web search. Cards now keep only web search and chat search; Hermux can still show a file's contents in a card from its own turn, where your approvals apply. Checked with the plugin's tests on a computer; not tried on a phone.
