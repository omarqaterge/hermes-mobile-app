- **A finished reply no longer sits under every step it took.** Once a turn is done, its tool calls, sub-agents and
  thinking fold into one line above the answer, such as "Worked 1m 20s · 14 steps". Tap it to see every step exactly as
  before, tap again to fold it. A step that failed is named on the line in red ("· 1 failed") and is the first thing you
  see when you open it. The turn that is still working never folds, and a turn that finishes while you read earlier text
  waits until you next open the chat, so nothing moves under you. Changed files, canvas documents, cards and pictures
  stay in view, and search and the scrubber open a folded turn to find what they look for. Checked in a desktop
  browser. Not yet tried on a phone.
- **A thought says how long it took.** "Thought for 12s", with its first line beside it on the same row, for replies you
  watched arrive. The numbers in the status line keep the same width as they count, so they no longer jitter.
