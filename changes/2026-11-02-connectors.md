- **Connectors: give Hermes your other services.** Settings → Connectors lists what each bot can reach outside the phone, with its state (Connected, Needs setup, Off). MCP servers: add one by its address (with an account sign-in that opens in the browser, or a token) or by the command that starts it, pick one from Hermes's catalog (Notion, Linear and others, asking for the keys they need), turn it on or off, test it, remove it. Tools with a key or an account (web search, browser, image and video generation, Hermes's own voice, X search, Home Assistant, Spotify): turn them on, pick the service and paste its key, or disconnect it. Keys for skills (Notion, Linear, Airtable, GitHub) have their own rows. Everything goes through Hermes's own settings, for new chats. Tested in the browser against the test Hermes and checked against the real Hermes's answers; not yet on a phone.
