- **Answer Hermux's questions from the top of the screen.** Tapping a question on the status chip, or its Answer
  button, now opens a dark panel that grows out of the top of the screen, where the camera is, over the app you are
  in: the chat's name, the question, one row per choice, a field for your own answer and Open chat. A tap on a choice
  or Send answers; a check shows it went through and the panel closes by itself. If the answer could not be sent, the
  panel says so and Open chat keeps your text. If the question was answered or closed elsewhere meanwhile, the panel
  says "This question is closed". Back, a tap outside or a swipe up closes it and leaves the question for later. With
  the phone's animations off it only fades in and out. It replaces the small answer box. Checked on Android 15 and
  Android 8.0 emulators; not yet tried on a phone.
