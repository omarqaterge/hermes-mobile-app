- **"New since your last visit" in a chat.** When a chat got messages while you were away (a long task finished, a
  scheduled job or a bot wrote to it), a thin line with that text sits before the first message you haven't seen, and
  the chat opens scrolled to it instead of to the very end. It goes away the next time you open the chat; a chat you
  never opened on this phone shows no line. It also works when you leave the app with the chat open (Home while Hermes answers) and come back: the first phone test found that case had no line, because the chat was never reopened. Seen on an Android 16 phone after the fix: the line before the reply that came while away, the chat scrolled to it, no line for a reply watched on screen, none after reopening.
