- **Frosted glass bars and sheets, and animations you can turn down.** The chat now scrolls under its header and
  message box, which are a soft frosted glass the conversation shows through; menus, dialogs and notices are glass too,
  and stay easy to read over a photo or a dark code block in every theme. Sheets spring up while the chat steps back,
  dialogs and notices leave gently, and buttons give a little under your finger. Settings → Appearance → Effects has
  Glass effects (solid bars instead) and Animations (Full, Reduced or Off); until you choose, they follow your phone,
  so "Remove animations" turns them off and a phone with little memory starts with solid bars and fewer animations.
  The bars keep their sizes, so no line of the chat is lost. Checked in a desktop browser (heights, contrast and
  speed); not yet tried on a phone.
