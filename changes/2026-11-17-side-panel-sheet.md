- **The chat list is now a panel that floats over your chat.** It no longer covers the whole screen: it is a sheet in
  frosted glass, a little in from the edge, and the chat stays in view round it, dimmed and stepped back. Swipe right
  to pull it in under your finger, tap the chat beside it or swipe left to put it away. At the top is the search field
  ("Search chats"); under it, scrolling with your chats, three rows: Scheduled (when the next job runs), Skills (how
  many are on) and Library. At the bottom, always in reach of your thumb: your bot, a Settings button and a gold New
  chat button. The three tiles and the Hermux page are gone; Files, Projects and the canvas gallery are now the three
  parts of the Library page, which remembers the part you used last. Tapping the bot at the bottom opens a sheet to
  switch bots, see what the bot remembers or manage your bots. On a tablet the list stays docked as before. Checked in
  a desktop browser at three phone widths and on a tablet width, with glass on and off and in all three themes. Not yet
  tried on a phone: the edge swipe against Android's own back gesture.
