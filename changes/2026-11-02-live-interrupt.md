- **Interrupt Hermes in Live mode.** While Hermes reads its answer aloud, just start talking: it stops and listens to
  you, and what you say becomes your next message (saying only "stop" or "wait" stops it without sending anything).
  The Live bar also has an Interrupt button. Live mode keeps the microphone on while Hermes speaks and ignores
  Hermes's own voice; other voices nearby, such as a TV, can cut it off too, so Settings → Voice → Live mode →
  Interrupt by talking turns it off. Seen on an Android 16 phone with the on-device speech model (the phone's echo
  canceller kept Hermes from hearing itself); not yet tried with the phone's own speech recognition or in a quiet room.
