- **See what changed right after you update.** The first time Hermux opens on a new version, a sheet over the chat
  lists what is new in it, one line per change, biggest first, and every version you skipped too. Details shows the
  full notes; Got it closes it for good. It stays away on a new install, when the notes can't be read (it tries again
  next start), and while Setup or a dialog is open. Checked in browser tests with a stand-in changelog; not yet seen
  on a phone after a real update.
