- **More of the chat on screen: the model bar tucks away.** The model, Auto, the
  context ring and the line that shows what Hermux is doing now sit in a tray above the message box. A new chat shows it
  open so you can pick the model, and it tucks away when you send the first message; a chat you open from the list
  starts with it tucked away. Tap the small handle above the message box, or drag it, to open or close it; it stays as
  you leave it while that chat is open. The handle glows gold while Hermux works, turns amber when Hermux waits for you,
  and takes the context ring's colour when the context is nearly full. On a small phone that gives the conversation
  about 33 more pixels, and about 60 while a reply is coming in. Prefer the old layout? Settings → Appearance → Model
  bar → Always open. Checked in a desktop browser; not yet tried on a phone.
