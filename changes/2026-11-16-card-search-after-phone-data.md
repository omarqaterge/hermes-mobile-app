- **Cards can't search the web in a chat that has read your phone data.** A card's live web search ran without asking, even after the agent had read your messages or contacts, so it could carry that data out in its search words. In such a chat a card now says it can't search the web; chat search in cards still works. Checked with unit and browser tests on a computer; not tried on a phone.
