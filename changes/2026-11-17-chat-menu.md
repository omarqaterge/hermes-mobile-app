- **One menu for a chat.** The chat's menu (the three dots at the top) and a chat's menu in the chat list now look
  the same and share their top: the chat's title with a pencil to rename it and, under it, the model (and the bot, when
  you have several). The menu at the top puts what you use most first, as four buttons: Search, Canvas (with how many
  documents the chat has), Share and Pin. Pin and Archive are now there too, so you no longer need the chat list for
  them; archiving the chat you are in starts a new one. Delete is last, in red, apart from the rest, and still asks
  first.
- **Read replies aloud is a switch.** It flips in place without closing the menu and says underneath whether the chat
  has its own choice ("Only this chat") or follows the setting for all chats. Undo last turn says why it waits while a
  reply runs.
- **Chat details in plain words.** Grouped into Chat, Model and Usage, with "Bot", "Working folder" and "Chat ID" (with
  a copy button) instead of "Profile", "Working dir" and "Session id". The menu of a new chat no longer offers Change
  model: the model button is right there on the screen. Checked in a desktop browser; not yet on a phone.
