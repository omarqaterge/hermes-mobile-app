- **Tap the chip to watch Hermes use an app.** While Hermes works in an app in the background, tapping its status
  notification opens Hermes on that chat with the live view of the app already open, where you can take over or
  stop it. If Hermes finished with the app just before the tap, the chat opens and says so. Checked in the browser
  tests (app closed, open on another chat, open on that chat); see the pull request for what was tried on a phone.
