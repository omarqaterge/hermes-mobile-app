- **Hermes is quicker and surer when it uses an app in the background.** It can go straight to the right screen (open a link in an app, or share text into it, such as a new note), point at buttons by what they say instead of by number, do several taps and typing in one go, scroll until it finds something, and wait for a page to finish loading. Each step answers as soon as the app stops changing instead of after a fixed pause, it sees the app's screen in a shorter, clearer list, and it can keep notes on how each app works for next time. It won't act on a button that has moved or changed since it looked. No keyboard is drawn on the hidden screen any more, the hidden app no longer hears about everything that happens on your own screen, and the live view stops re-encoding a picture that hasn't changed, all of which saves battery. Checked on an Android 16 phone in Settings (reading, marks, scrolling to an item, tapping by text, searching); not yet through Hermes itself, nor on other makers' phones.
