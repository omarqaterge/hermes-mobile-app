- **See what your sub-agents are doing.** Helpers a chat starts now fold into one line in the chat. Tap to list them, and
  tap one to see its goal, the model it runs on, its reasoning level and its live output. You can send it a message, stop
  it, or carry its work into a new chat. Approvals still come to the chat's own request sheet, which now says which
  sub-agent asks. Checked in a desktop browser against a test copy of Hermux. Not yet tried on a phone.
- **Sub-agents show under their chat in the chat list.** A chat with helpers shows them as rows beneath it while they run,
  and folds to "3 sub-agents" when they finish. Tap a row to open the chat with that helper's details, and search finds
  them too. Checked in a desktop browser against a test copy of Hermux. Not yet tried on a phone.
