- **Settings is shorter and says what needs you first.** The main page is now two short lists: Hermux (Models,
  Connectors, Behaviour, Access & privacy) and This app (Appearance, Voice, About & help), each row showing its current
  value, such as your model, the Auto mode or the theme. Above them a card shows what needs attention: no model
  connected yet (Add a provider), Hermux offline (Reconnect) or an update ready. "Hermux settings" is now called
  Behaviour and opens on Replies, with Dynamic UI and a way to see what Hermux remembers. Approvals, Phone data,
  Background apps and the diagnostic log are together under Access & privacy, with the same fingerprint and
  confirmations as before. Updates, help, versions and Uninstall are under About & help. Read every reply aloud is
  under Voice. Checked in a desktop browser at phone widths with a wider font, in all three themes. Not yet tried on a
  phone.
