- **Text follows your phone's font size, and you choose which notifications you get.** Until you pick a size in
  Settings → Appearance → Text size, Hermux now uses the font size set in Android (between 90 % and 135 %); the new
  first choice, Phone default, goes back to it. A new Settings → Notifications page has two switches: When a task
  finishes (a reply, a scheduled job or a background task is done) and When Hermux needs me (an approval or a
  question). With the second off, an approval or a question waits silently in the app until you open it, with the same
  fingerprint and unlock rules as before. The page also opens Android's sound, vibration and lock-screen settings for
  Hermux, and says when Android has the app's notifications turned off, with a button to turn them on. In the Light
  theme the gold and the grey text are darker, so small words are easier to read. Checked in a desktop browser at
  phone widths and in all three themes; not yet tried on a phone.
