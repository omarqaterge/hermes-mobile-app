### 3.2 Chat screen

The chat screen is the home of the app: what the user sees when no `screen` is open. It is made of the pieces in the table. Everything lives in `web/src/App.tsx` unless another file is named.

#### Bars (the chat scrolls under them)

The rows above the messages (test-build strip excepted: header, local authentication alert, connection banner, find
bar) sit in one bar, `.bar-top`; the rows below them (activity banner, status bar, compress hint, selection bar,
read-aloud player, background-app card, composer) in another, `.bar-bottom`. Both carry the glass material (`.glass`,
3.20 Glass) and float over the list: `.stage` holds the two bars and `.chat-wrap`, and the list (`.chat`) reaches under
both bars, so the glass has the chat behind it. `watchBars` (`barinsets.ts`) measures the bars with a
`ResizeObserver` and writes their heights as `--bar-top` and `--bar-bottom` (registered with `@property` as not
inherited, so a bar changing height restyles only the list, the scroll button and the pull hint, never every message);
the list's padding and `scroll-padding` use them, so the first message starts below the header, the last one ends above
the composer, and a jump never puts its target under a bar. Code that scrolls to a place subtracts the top bar
(`barInsets()`): the visit divider, a find hit, the scrubber's pick, keeping the reader at the end when the bottom bar
grows (`onBarInsets`). The layout and every height are the same as before the bars were glass (header 57 px, composer
area 101 px, messages 582 px at 360 × 740; `docs/decisions/chat-height-belongs-to-content.md`). With Glass effects off
the bars are solid and nothing else changes. Since the composer's tray (3.4 Tray), the bottom bar is 69 px while the
tray is closed (messages 614 px, 314 px with the keyboard open), idle and while a reply runs; open it keeps the heights
above (122 px while a reply runs, 130 before). The tray's handle (`TrayHandle`) is the bottom bar's last child, placed
on its top edge.

#### The empty new-chat screen

Shown when there is no open chat and none is opening (`Empty`).

- The Hermux mark (the launcher icon's caduceus and arrows, an inline SVG from `HERMUX_MARK` in `hermux-mark.ts` in `web/src/components`, in the accent colour; `tools/icon/make-icon.cjs` generates it), the title "Hermux", and a line: "Your agent, on your phone." for the default profile, or "Profile: <name>".
- **No model yet:** while the bot has no working model (`useModelReady` in `web/src/model-ready.ts` answers no: Hermes's `setup.runtime_check`, asked once per bot while the connection is open and again after a provider was connected or a model chosen, never on a timer), a notice under the line, a solid card (role `group`, name "No model connected yet"): "No model connected yet" and the gold button "Connect a model", which opens Settings on Add a provider (`openSettingsOn`; Back returns to the chat). The mark and the title do not move: the notice has a fixed height (`--empty-notice-h`) and `.has-notice` adds a spacer of the same height above the mark, so the centred column grows evenly; when the screen is too short to centre (keyboard open) the spacer shrinks away and the column starts at the top as before.
- Up to four suggestion buttons, built by `suggestions()` in order, without repeats: the three latest prompts this profile sent (`web/src/recent.ts` `recentPrompts`, shortened to 70 characters), then up to two skills ("/<skill> · <description>", from the command catalog, `commands.ts`), then four built-in suggestions (calendar today, summarise unread email, a quiz prompt, files in Downloads).
- Tapping a suggestion does not send it. It puts the text into the composer (a trailing space added) and focuses it.
- Nothing is created on Hermes's side until the first message (or the first attachment, a model pick, or an Auto choice).

#### While a chat opens (skeleton and preview)

`store.opening` holds the stored id of a chat being resumed.

- If the app has the chat's last-seen items (`web/src/chatcache.ts`: 8 chats in memory, the last one also in `hm.chatcache.v1`), they are drawn at once as a read-only preview: no message actions, no editing. The title is the cached title.
- If not, a skeleton appears (aria-label Opening your chat): a spinner with the chat's title (or "Opening your chat…", or "Connecting to Hermux…" when offline) over four grey bubble shapes.
- When `session.resume` answers, the real items replace the preview.
- `web/src/chatview.ts` gives every component the same answer to "which chat is on screen" from `opening`, `preview` and `active`. `viewKind` is `home` (the new-chat screen), `open`, `preview` or `loading` (the skeleton); a chat reloading after a reconnect with its messages still on screen stays `open`. `shownItems`, `shownKey` and `shownTitle` are the list's messages, the id its scroll position is kept under, and the header title; `screenChat` is the stored id the canvas and the cards' saved answers belong to (the chat opening, else the open one).
- In `preview` and `loading` the store's `active` is still the chat being left (or empty at a cold start), so `viewSettled` is false and what acts on `active` waits: Chat options and the Auto button are disabled, and the Auto button shows Off. The header shows the title of the chat that is opening (the cached one, or the one in the chat list), never the title of the chat being left.

#### Windowing, folding, search jump

- **Windowing.** Only the last 40 items (`WINDOW`) are drawn when a chat opens, because every item parses Markdown. At the top, an "Earlier messages…" line with a spinner is watched by an `IntersectionObserver` with an 800 px margin. When it comes near, the 40 items before are drawn and the scroll position is kept on the message that was first. New items arriving do not move the start index.
- **Opening at the divider.** If a chat has a "New Messages" divider above the last screenful, it opens scrolled to the divider instead of the end (see 3.2, New Messages).
- **Stick to the end.** While the user is within 80 px of the end, the list follows new content. Scrolling further up stops the following and shows the scroll button. Switching chat sticks again.
- **Folded tool runs.** `web/src/fold.ts` folds three or more tool cards in a row (`FOLD_MIN`) into one line (see 3.3).
- **Folded turns.** `web/src/turnfold.ts` folds a finished turn's work into one line above its answer ("Worked 1m 20s · 14 steps", see 3.3). The running turn never folds, and one that finishes on screen folds only when that moves nothing the reader sees.
- **Jump from message search.** The drawer's message search sets `store.jump` (`web/src/jump.ts`: chat id, the marked words, the snippet). After the chat opens, `findHit` picks the message whose text holds the most marked words and longest snippet pieces (ties go to the latest), draws earlier items if the hit is above the window, opens a folded turn and then a folded run if the hit is inside one, scrolls it to the centre, flashes it for 2.5 s and marks the words for 6 s with the CSS Custom Highlight API (`highlightWords`, name `hm-hit`). If no message matches, a toast says "Opened the chat; the exact message wasn’t found".
- **In-chat search.** `findMatches` lists the items whose text contains the query (case-insensitive), newest first. All hits in drawn messages are marked (`hm-find`), the current one (`hm-hit`) is centred, scrolling only when the query or the chosen match changes. Searched text: user and assistant text, and for tool cards their context and result text.
- **Scrubber jump.** Letting go on the chat scrubber (3.2) sets `scrubTo` (an item index) in `Chat`: it draws earlier items first when the message is above the window, opens a folded turn or run if needed (`unfold`), scrolls the message to 8 px below the top, and flashes it for 2.5 s: the same steps as the search jump. The thumb follows the scroll (`trackTop`, once per animation frame): each of your messages' `offsetTop` in the list (0 for those above the drawn window) and the line from `readLine` (10 px under the top of the screen, sliding down to the end of the list over the last screenful) give `railPosition`, the position counted in messages (2.5 is half way from your third message to your fourth). `paintRail` writes it to the rail's `--thumb` and `--merge` CSS variables without a re-render: within a quarter of a gap of a tick the thumb is pulled onto it and shrinks into the tick's shape. The whole part of the position is the current message (`aria-valuenow`, ArrowUp/ArrowDown); part way through a reply ArrowUp first goes back to the start of that message. While a finger is on the rail the thumb sits on the picked tick.

#### Auto button (composer, beside the model chip)

`web/src/components/AutoAccess.tsx` `AutoButton`. It sits in the composer's model row (in the tray, 3.4), right after the model chip, with the same chip shape (28 px high, rounded). It sets how much Hermes may do in this chat without asking. Three states:

| State | Button | aria-label starts with | What Hermes does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Off | Grey "Auto" | "Auto is off" | Asks before risky commands (the approval sheet, 3.6). |
| Auto access | Green "Auto" | "Auto access is on" | Runs everything without asking, except commands on Hermes's dangerous list (recursive deletes, overwriting system or config files, force-killing processes, piping downloads into a shell…). Those are blocked by the plugin's `auto_mode.py`. |
| Full access | Red "⚡ Full access" | "Full access is on" | Runs every command without asking. Only Hermes's catastrophic list (wipe the disk, shut down) stays blocked. |

Every label ends with ". Change how much Hermux may do without asking". While another chat opens the button shows Off and is disabled (`viewSettled`, see "While a chat opens").

- **Which state it shows.** With a chat open: `accessOf(active)` = off when `info.yolo` is false, Auto access when it is true and `autoAccess` is set, else Full. While a chat is opening: off. On the empty screen: `defaultAccess()`, the mode a new chat will start in (Settings, row Auto for all chats, key `hm.defaultAccess.v1`).
- **Tap** opens the picker "Auto: run without asking" with the three options (Off, Auto access, Full access) and a check on the current one. Choosing the current mode does nothing.
- **Full access asks first** (`confirmFullAccess` in `access-confirm.ts`, which `/approvals off` and `smart` share, 3.4). A dialog titled "Give Hermux full access?" warns that every command runs without asking (deleting or overwriting files, killing apps, installing software, changing settings, sending data) and that nothing is checked first. Buttons: Cancel and "Allow full access" (red). Declining changes nothing.
- **On the empty screen** the choice starts the chat first (`newSession`), because access is a property of a chat.
- **Setting it** (`setAccess` in `gateway.ts`; one chat's switches run one at a time through `accessRuns`, and the newest request wins: one still waiting is dropped (`SUPERSEDED`, it answers with the newer one's result) and one already switching to Auto access stops before turning `yolo` on (removing the plugin entry it just wrote unless the chat was in Auto access), so a `/yolo` typed while the chat's start-up switch (Settings → Auto for all chats) still runs applies right after the step in progress instead of after the whole switch):
  - Auto access: `PUT /auto` (plugin writes the chat id to its block list) first, then `config.set` key `yolo` = `1`. If Hermes does not turn yolo on, the plugin entry is removed again. An old plugin without `/auto` gives the error "Auto access needs the updated Hermux plugin".
  - Full access: `config.set` `yolo` = `1`, and the plugin entry is removed.
  - Off: `config.set` `yolo` = `0`, and the plugin entry is removed.
  - Order matters: a chat is never briefly in Full access by accident.
  - A `config.set` `yolo` that fails or times out may still have been applied, and Hermes has no call to read the flag back, so `settleAccess` ends the switch where the screen is never safer than the chat: it sends `yolo` = `0` and removes the plugin entry (Off); when Hermes does not confirm Off, it writes the plugin entry back so the chat is in Auto access at most and shows that, or Full access when that write fails too. It never gives the phone-data pass, keeps the remembered mode, and `accessNotice` adds an error notice to the chat from `ACCESS_UNSURE` ("Couldn’t change this chat’s access, so it is back to Off…", "Couldn’t confirm this chat’s access, so it is in Auto access…", "Couldn’t confirm this chat’s access: it may be in Full access…"). The switch's own error still shows as before.
  - A toast confirms: "Auto off: Hermux asks before risky commands again", "Auto access on for this chat: dangerous commands are blocked, everything else runs", or "Full access on for this chat: Hermux runs commands without asking". Errors show as an error toast.
- **Remembering.** The user's own choice is stored per chat in `hm.yolo.v1` (`true` = Full, `'auto'` = Auto access, `false` = an explicit Off that beats the default). Hermes forgets yolo when a chat is resumed, so `resumeSession` puts it back from `hm.yolo.v1`, or from the default for a chat with no entry. A resumed chat remembered in Auto access that Hermes still has in yolo only rewrites the plugin entry; if that `PUT /auto` fails, the chat is switched to Off (not remembered) with the notice "Auto access couldn’t be set again, so this chat is in Off: Hermux asks before risky commands. Tap Auto to try again." The default is applied with `remember` false, so it keeps following the default. New chats get the default in `newSession`.
- **One chip, not two.** In Full access the button itself reads "⚡ Full access" in red, so it is also the warning that commands run unchecked; there is no separate Full access chip. Turning it off is two taps: the button, then Off.
- **Where it shows.** With the model row, in the composer's tray (open on a new chat, closed on a chat from the list, 3.4 Tray); hidden while the slash menu is open (3.4). In Live mode, where the model row gives way to the Live bar, the Live bar shows it instead as an icon-only chip (`compact`: 🛡 or ⚡), but only in Auto access or Full access (`hideWhenOff`), so hands-free commands never run unchecked without a sign on screen. It never shrinks; on a narrow phone the model name ellipsizes first.
- **`/yolo` command.** Typing `/yolo` in the composer switches access the same way (`setYolo`) and adds a notice to the chat naming the mode it is in now (`YOLO_NOTICE`): "⚡ Full access on for this chat (/yolo): Hermux runs commands without asking", "Back to Auto access: dangerous commands are blocked, everything else runs" or "Auto off: Hermux asks before risky commands again". `/yolo on` = Full access, `/yolo off` = Off. A bare `/yolo` goes to Full access from Off or Auto access; from Full access it goes back to the mode the chat was in before Full access (`beforeFull`, kept in memory per chat and set whenever a chat enters Full access by `/yolo` or the button), or Off when that is unknown (after an app restart, or a chat that started in Full access). Over the lock screen, and when Auto access can't be set (an older plugin), it goes to Off instead, so leaving Full access always works.
- **Settings row.** `DefaultAccessRow` in the same file is the Settings row Auto for all chats: a `SelectRow` with the same three options. Choosing Full asks "Full access in every chat?" (button "Allow in all chats"). Off removes the key.

#### New Messages

`web/src/visit.ts` keeps, per chat, when it was last on screen (`hm.visit.v1`, ms, newest 500 chats). `markVisit` runs in `gateway.ts` when the user leaves a chat (another chat opens or a new draft starts), when the app goes away while a chat is open (`noteAway`: the shell's `window.hermesPause` or the page's `visibilitychange`, whichever comes first), and when a turn ends (`message.complete`) while the page is visible and not away (`isAway`). When `Chat` shows a chat whose key changed it reads `lastVisit` once (a `visitRef`), and the first time the chat's own items (not the read-only preview) are there it asks `firstUnseen` for the first user or assistant item with `at` newer than that time (never index 0; items without `at`, such as tool cards, are skipped). That index is frozen for as long as the chat stays open, so new live messages do not move it. The item is drawn with a `visit-divider` line (`role="separator"`, text "New Messages", accent colour) before it, inside a `visit-wrap` element; when the item is also in a turn-end or tool-run wrapper, the divider is the first child of that wrapper, so there is still one child of `.chat` per item. A chat never opened on this phone has no divider.

**Away and back with the same chat open.** Leaving the app (Home, another app, screen off) and coming back keeps the chat on screen, so it is not reopened. `noteAway` records which chat was open and when; `noteBack` (from `window.hermesResume` or `visibilitychange`, whichever comes first) tells `Chat` through `onBack`/`lastBack`. If that chat is still the one shown, `Chat` looks, as items arrive, for the first user or assistant item stamped after the leave time and no later than `BACK_GRACE_MS` (5 s) after the return (`newWhileAway`: messages from the backlog are stamped when the page processes them). It stops looking 10 s after the return. When one is found, the divider moves there (replacing an older one) and the chat scrolls to it under the same rule as when opening. Replies watched on screen get no divider.

Initial scroll: when the divider is above the last screenful, the chat opens with it 8 px below the top instead of at the end (earlier items are drawn first if it is above the window, like a search jump), and the scroll button shows. A message-search jump or an in-chat search that is waiting takes priority and no divider scroll happens. Only once per opened chat; the user's own scrolling is never overridden afterwards.

#### Unread chats

`web/src/unread.ts` keeps, per profile, the message count the user last saw in each chat and when the count last changed (`hm.seen.v1.<profile>`). After every `session.list`, `noteSessions` updates it. A chat is unread when it has more messages than were seen; the open chat is read while the app is on screen (`appInBackground()` false). A reply that lands in the open chat while the app is in the background leaves it unread, so the service's sticky Done chip is not removed by the page's next list; when the page becomes visible again (`visibilitychange`) the open chat is marked seen. The first list ever is the baseline (nothing unread); a chat that started after tracking began and has messages counts as unread. `markSeen` runs when a chat opens. The unread list is shown as the dot on the menu button and in the drawer, and mirrored to the Android service (`setUnseen` bridge call) for its Done chip and the icon badge. `ago()` formats the drawer's "5 min" labels, and `lastActive` (when the count last changed) is the chat list's last activity, which orders it (3.13). At most 1500 chats are tracked.

#### Activity feed

`web/src/activity.ts` polls the plugin's `GET /activity` while the page is visible and connected: every 3 s while the banner shows something, once 0.3 s after the open chat's turn ends (the background review starts then), otherwise on every third tick (9 s). `bannerItems` hides the open chat's own live turn (it has the status bar), but keeps its background review. When a chat drops out of the list, the session list is refreshed so its unread dot appears. The same answer's `waiting` list goes to `store.waiting` ("Needs you", 3.13); it does not make the feed poll faster. When a request leaves `store.requests`, its chat leaves `store.waiting` at once and the feed is read once more 0.3 s later.

#### Agent motion

for the shared pieces, `web/src/agent-motion.css` for the movement; owner's wish in `docs/decisions/glass-and-motion.md`,
