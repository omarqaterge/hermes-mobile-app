All sources are under `android/src/com/omarqaterge/hermesmobile` (package `com.omarqaterge.hermesmobile`), plus `bg/BgHost.java`, the phone-data readers in `phonedata/`, and `android/src/moe/shizuku/api/BinderContainer.java`. | `LocalClient` | The client half of local authentication over HTTP. `open(role, port, keys)` dials the literal `127.0.0.1` (`HOST`, no name lookup), sends `GET /hermux/hello?v=1&nc=<nonce>` and checks the answer: status 200 on HTTP/1.1 with one `Content-Length`, no `Transfer-Encoding`, no `Connection: close` and nothing after the body, at most `MAX_HELLO` (4 KiB) in all; JSON (strict, a repeated name refused, by the small reader `Json`) with `hermux` at least `MIN_VERSION`, the expected `role`, a nonce-shaped `ns`, `addr` equal to the address dialled, a `kid` the `KeySet` (`byKid`) knows, and `proof` equal to the `server` MAC over the client's nonce and the dialled address.
