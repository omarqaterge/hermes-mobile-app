### 3.13 Side panel + chat list

The side panel is the left-hand panel with the search field, three rows (Scheduled, Skills, Library), the list of chats and a bar with the bot, Settings and New chat. On a phone it is a sheet of the glass material that lies over the chat; on a wide screen it is the docked column. It is always mounted (`App.tsx` renders `Drawer`); the `drawer` flag in the store (`web/src/store.ts`) opens it. Decision: [side-panel-layout.md](../decisions/side-panel-layout.md).

**Source files:** `web/src/components/Drawer.tsx` (`Drawer`, `BotSheet`), `web/src/panel.css` (its look), `web/src/panelvalues.ts` (the rows' values), `web/src/components/SessionList.tsx` (`SessionList`, `SwipeRow`, `RowLine`, `RunSource`, `useDrag`, `SessionMenu`), `web/src/chatorder.ts` (`orderChats`, `recentIds`, `dayLabel` and the snapshot helpers), `web/src/chatsnapshot.ts` (when the order is read: `takeChatSnapshot`, `useChatSnapshot`), `web/src/order.ts` (`useOrder`, `updateOrder`), `web/src/unread.ts` (`ago`, `lastActive`: the "time ago" text and each chat's last activity), `drawerdrag.ts` (the panel following the finger on a swipe).

#### Side panel
**Opens from:** the Sessions button in the chat header (`aria-label` "Sessions", or "Sessions (unread chats)" when a chat finished while you were elsewhere); a swipe right on a chat, which slides it in under the finger (`web/src/swipe.ts`, `web/src/drawerdrag.ts`; 3.1 has the rules). **Closes with:** a tap on the dimmed chat beside it (the backdrop), a swipe left on the panel (not on a chat row) or beside it, which pushes it back under the finger, Android Back (`App.tsx` handles Back: it closes the panel first), opening a chat, New chat, Settings, or one of the three rows. **Source:** `web/src/components/Drawer.tsx` `Drawer` (an `aside`, name "Chats").

**The sheet (phones, and wide screens while the column is hidden):** 8 px in from the left edge, 8 px below the top safe area and 8 px above the bottom safe area, `min(80vw, 330px)` wide (256 px on a 320 px phone), corners 24 px, so a strip of the chat stays visible on the right and the chat shows round the other edges. It is made of the glass material (`web/src/glass.css` tokens; the blur is the sheet's `::before`, so the sheet's own box stays the frame of what opens inside it). It is the one glass surface: the search field and the bottom bar are faint solid tints (`--glass-field`, `--glass-group`) with a hairline, and rows are plain. With Glass effects off, or a WebView without backdrop blur, it is solid `--surface` with its border and shadow in the same place and size. It is laid out once and moves by `transform`; its blur never changes size. Closed, it sits off the screen (its shadow too), as the drawer did before. It is neither hidden with `visibility` nor made `inert`: each made opening the list cost the main thread more than the slide itself (80 rows painted or restyled again; docs/performance-history.md). The chat behind it stays where it is, dims (`.drawer-backdrop`, `--scrim`) and, with Full animations, steps back (`scale` 0.965, transform only); under Reduced and Off the scrim alone dims it. While it is open the bars behind it stop blurring, so it is the only blurred surface drawn fresh. With the search field focused and the keyboard up, the sheet's bottom edge sits 8 px above the keyboard (the window shrinks) and the list still scrolls. On a wide screen (`min-width: 840px`) with the column docked, the same element is the docked column: `--side` (320 px) wide from the top-left corner, full height, square corners, solid, no backdrop and no step back; the hide button (`side-hide`, `aria-label` "Hide the chat list") sits beside the search field. Hidden by the person, it opens as the sheet above.

The three rows are the first items of the scrolling area (`.drawer-list`): they scroll away with the chats while the search field and the bar stay. A chat row can be indented later (nested rows are not built yet).

**The values** (`web/src/panelvalues.ts`): `refreshPanelValues` reads `GET /api/cron/jobs` and `GET /api/skills` (the calls the old hub page made) when the panel opens (on a phone once its slide has ended, `transitionend`, or at once under Reduced and Off, so the answer never lands in the middle of the slide; docked on a wide screen: once at start and when the app returns to the foreground, `window.hermesResume`), unless the values for this bot are under a minute old (`PANEL_VALUES_TTL_MS`). There is no timer; nothing is read while the panel is closed. A bot switch clears them. The same jobs list also gives the job names (`namesOf`, id to name) that a scheduled run's row shows (`useJobName`, Session list below).

**Bot sheet** (`BotSheet`, a bottom sheet in the shared look, title "Bots"): opens from the bar's bot button, over the chat once the panel has closed (the panel is closed first and the sheet follows after its slide, so a glass sheet never lies on the glass panel). One row per bot (avatar letter, display name or id, its model in dim text; the current one is `aria-pressed` with a check): a tap closes the sheet and switches to that bot (`switchProfile` in `web/src/gateway.ts` stores the id in `hm.profile`, resets the page size to 80 chats, shows the cached list for that bot (`hm.sessions.v1.<profile>`), then loads the list and the default model from Hermes). Under them: "What <bot> remembers" (opens Memory, 3.14) and "Manage bots" (opens Bots, 3.16). With one bot the list is that bot and the two rows are what the sheet is for.

**Data:** reads `drawer`, `profiles`, `profile` from the store. The bot list comes from the `profiles.list` RPC (`loadProfiles`).
**Rules:** the three tiles, the Hermux hub page and the top bot switcher are gone; Back from Scheduled, Skills, Library, Memory, Bots and Settings goes to the chat. There is no messaging-channel entry anywhere.

#### Session list
**Opens from:** inside the side panel. **Source:** `web/src/components/SessionList.tsx` `SessionList`.

A session row shows, from left to right:
- an unread dot (`aria-label` "Finished, not opened yet") when the chat has more messages than you last saw, it is not the open chat and it is not working;
- 📌 for a pinned chat;
- the title (via `Title`, which also renders the branch marker), else the first-message preview, else "Untitled". While the chat is running (the open chat's own turn, or any chat in the plugin's activity feed; a background memory/skills review does not count) a soft band of light sweeps across the title line (`.session-title.working`, keyframes `working-shimmer`) and screen readers hear "Working". The band is a mask over the line, so the 📌, the branch icon and any emoji keep their colours. It redraws 24 times a second, is paused while the chat list is closed on a phone (the docked list on a wide screen keeps it running), and with reduced motion the line stays still and dimmed;
- under it, one line in dim text (`RowLine`, `.session-sub`: it never wraps; a long source is cut with an ellipsis): the time since last activity (now, N min, N h, N d, N wk, or a date after about two months); then a state word when there is one: "Working" while the chat runs (the same rule as the title's band of light, which stays) or "Finished" while it is unread (the dot stays), nothing otherwise; then where the chat came from when not from this app (`source` other than `mobile`). A scheduled job's run (`source` `cron`) shows a clock icon (`role="img"`, name "Scheduled") and the job's name instead of the word `cron` (`RunSource`): the name from the jobs list that the side panel reads when it opens (`useJobName`, `web/src/panelvalues.ts`, no extra call) when the run's chat id names its job (`cronJobId`: the pinned Hermes calls a run's chat `cron_<job id>_<YYYYmmdd>_<HHMMSS>`), else the start of the run's title (Hermes titles a run "<job name> · Nov 17 09:00"; `runName`), else "Scheduled". Parts are joined with ` · `. The state word is text, so the state does not rest on the dot's colour; screen readers hear the state once, before the title (the dot's name "Finished, not opened yet", `role="img"`, or the hidden "Working"), and the visible word is `aria-hidden`. The line used to show ` · N msgs`, the messages you sent (from the plugin's `POST /sent-counts`); it is gone and the app no longer asks for the counts (the plugin keeps the route, 5.4);
- a ⋮ button on the right (`aria-label` "Chat options") that opens the item menu. It stops the pointer from starting a swipe or drag.

**Row gestures** (a row is a `SwipeRow`; touch only, no handles, no edit mode):
| Gesture | What happens |
|---|---|
| Tap | Haptic, `resumeSession(id)` opens the chat. A toast shows any error. A tap that moved more than 6 px is ignored. |
| Swipe right | The row slides and a blue "Archive" strip (🗄) shows behind it (label "Unarchive" in the archived list). Past 30 % of the row width the strip is armed and the phone ticks. Release past it: the row slides out and the chat is archived (toast "Archived"; "Restored" when unarchiving). Release earlier: it springs back (220 ms). |
| Swipe left | A red "Delete" strip (🗑) shows. Past 30 % of the width it is armed. Release past it: the row springs back and a confirmation dialog asks `Delete “<title>”?` ("The whole conversation is removed. This cannot be undone.", red Delete button). Only after the user confirms is the chat deleted (toast "Deleted"). |
| Swipe detection | A horizontal move of more than 10 px that is at least 1.3 times the vertical move starts the swipe (the pointer is captured). A vertical move of more than 10 px is a scroll. The row follows the finger up to 90 % of its width. |
| Long-press, then move | After 450 ms without moving, the phone vibrates and the row lifts. Keep the finger down and move: the row follows the finger, the others slide aside to show the gap, and the list auto-scrolls when the finger is within 72 px of the top or bottom edge of the list. Release to drop it in place. Nothing is saved while dragging: the new order is committed on release. A long-press released without moving does nothing. |
| Drag a chat up into Pinned | Drag past the bottom edge of the Pinned block (the block is highlighted and a gap opens where it will land). Release: the chat is pinned at that spot (toast "Pinned"). |
| Drag a pinned chat down out of Pinned | Drag below the block (the heading says "· release to unpin"). Release: the chat is unpinned and lands at that spot in the Chats list (a custom order is created if there was none; toast "Unpinned"). |
| Drag inside a list | Pinned: sets the pin order. Chats: while the drag is lifted the date groups merge into one flat "Chats" list so a chat can cross days; on release the new order is stored as the custom order. Dropping it back where it was restores the date groups. |

Reordering is switched off while the search field has text, and archived rows cannot be dragged. Dragging a chat to Pinned works only from the Chats list.

**Item menu** (⋮ button): a bottom sheet (`SessionMenu`, dismissable by the grip like every sheet) with the chat title and these entries:
| Entry | What it does |
|---|---|
| Rename | Swaps the list for a field "Chat title" and a Save button. Saving calls `renameStored` (`PATCH /api/sessions/<id>`), keeps any branch marker in the title (`keepBranch`) and toasts "Renamed". |
| Pin to top / Unpin | Not shown for archived chats. Pin puts the chat first in Pinned (toast "Pinned"); Unpin removes it (toast "Unpinned"). |
| Archive / Unarchive | Archive calls the `session.set_hidden` RPC with `hidden: true` (the same flag Hermes Desktop uses), drops the chat from Pinned, and starts a new draft if it was the open chat. Unarchive sets it to false. |
| Delete | Same confirmation as the left swipe. Deleting closes the chat's live sessions first (Hermes refuses to delete a live one), calls `session.delete`, removes it from Pinned and the custom order, and asks the plugin to clean up the chat's attachments (`POST /cleanup`). |

**Order and pins.** Decision: [chats-ordered-by-last-activity.md](../decisions/chats-ordered-by-last-activity.md). `web/src/chatorder.ts` `orderChats` sorts the unpinned chats: with no custom order, by last activity, newest first (then the newer creation, then the id); with a custom order, the saved positions, and any chat missing from it goes on top, newest created first, as before. Last activity is `lastActive` (the time the chat's message count last changed, tracked in `hm.seen.v1.<profile>`, else its start time), but read from a snapshot so that rows never move while someone looks at them: `web/src/chatsnapshot.ts` keeps each listed chat's activity time (`activityTimes`) as of the latest snapshot, and the list sorts and groups by those frozen times (`frozenActivity`; the date headings as of the snapshot's time too). `takeChatSnapshot` reads the times again only:
- when the panel starts to open: the `drawer` flag turning on (the menu button, anything else that opens it), or a swipe that starts pulling it in (`panelOpenGestureStarted` from `web/src/drawerdrag.ts`, before the panel comes into view; `panelOpenGestureEnded` when the swipe ends, so its own `drawer` change takes no second one);
- when the first list from Hermes arrives after start (the one before it may come from the cache), and when the bot changes;
- when a chat is created: a new list holds a chat made less than five minutes before the last snapshot or after it (`CREATED_MARGIN_MS`);
- when a chat is deleted, archived or restored from the list (`chatListChanged`; a restored chat comes back with the next list, which takes the snapshot);
- while the list is docked on a wide screen: when another chat is opened, and when the app returns to the foreground (`onAppResume`, from `window.hermesResume`).
It is never taken while a finger (or a mouse button or pen) is down on the panel, followed by touch events so a scroll counts as down, nor while a row is lifted for a drag (`holdChatSnapshot`): a snapshot asked for then is taken when the finger lifts or the row is dropped. Nothing else re-sorts the rows: a message sent or a reply arriving changes the row's time text and state word, not its place. A list that adds chats without one of the moments above (a longer page from "Show older chats", or an old chat that a new message brought into the page of 80, which also pushes the oldest out) keeps the rows that were there in place (`mergeListed`): a chat no newer than every chat in the snapshot joins at its own time (at the bottom), any other at its creation time, until the next snapshot. A snapshot with the same chats, times, bot and day as the last one (`sameTimes`) changes nothing and renders nothing, so opening the panel costs no render when nothing moved. There is no timer. The order is of top-level chats; rows that will later be nested under a chat (sub-agents, background tasks) belong inside that chat's own row container, so they move with it. `recentIds` builds the "Sort by recent" snapshot from `lastActive` right now and stores it as a custom order. `web/src/order.ts` keeps `{ pinned, manual, at }` per profile under the key family `hm.order.v1` (stored as `hm.order.v1.<profile>`), and mirrors it to the phone with `PUT /prefs` (debounced 1 s). Once per profile and run it reads `GET /prefs`: the copy with the newer `at` wins (the local copy is uploaded when it is newer). Both are routes of the Hermux plugin under `/api/plugins/hermes-mobile`.

**Search.** The field filters chats by title and first-message preview (case-insensitive substring). With 2 or more characters it also calls `GET /api/sessions/search?q=…&limit=25` after a 350 ms pause for the "In messages" group.

**Data:** RPCs `session.list` (page of 80, then +100), `session.set_hidden`, `session.delete`; archived list = `session.list` with `include_hidden` minus the normal list (limit 200 each); `GET /prefs` / `PUT /prefs`; local keys `hm.order.v1.<profile>`, `hm.seen.v1.<profile>`, `hm.sessions.v1.<profile>`.
**Rules (owner decisions, do not undo):** swipe right = Archive, swipe left = Delete with a confirm, the ⋮ menu has Pin / Archive / Delete (and Rename), reorder is long-press-drag-release with no mode and no handles, pinning is dragging into Pinned. Pin-by-swipe, long-press menus and a rearrange mode were rejected.
